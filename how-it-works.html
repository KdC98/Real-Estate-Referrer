How it works

Introduce a buyer, seller or tenant in Dubai — and get rewarded on every deal we close. No licence required.

1

Sign up (2 minutes)

Create your free account, fill in your details, and sign the referrer agreement online. No licence needed — the program is open to everyone.

2

Find a buyer, seller or tenant

Talk to your network. Anyone in Dubai looking to buy, sell or rent a property is a potential lead — wherever you are in the world.

3

Submit your lead

Log in to your dashboard, click "Add Lead", choose the type, and fill in your contact's details. That's all we need to get started.

4

Track in real time

Follow your lead's progress through each stage — New, Visit, Offer, Closed — with full transparency on your reward.

5

Get paid

Once the deal closes, your reward is calculated automatically and paid within 45–60 days — up to 25% of our agent commission for a buyer, 20% for a rental, or a flat AED 1,000 for a property you brought to sell.

What makes a good lead?

Qualified lead

  • •Genuinely wants to buy, sell or rent in Dubai
  • •Has a clear budget or property in mind
  • •Ready to move now, not in two years
  • •Easy to reach with accurate contact details
  • •Knows what they're looking for

Unqualified lead

  • •Just curious, not seriously interested
  • •No idea of budget or price, or unrealistic
  • •Looking far into the future (1 year+)
  • •Unreachable or fake contact details
  • •Doesn't know what they want

Frequently asked questions

What kind of leads can I refer?

Buyers, sellers, owners looking to rent out their property, and tenants looking to rent — all four are rewarded.

How many leads can I submit?

Unlimited. The more you introduce, the more you earn.

Do I need a real estate licence?

No. The program is open to everyone.

When do I get paid?

Within 45–60 days after the deal is finalised.

What if the deal doesn't happen?

No problem — you lose nothing and can keep introducing other contacts.
