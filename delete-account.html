Delete your account

Real Estate Referrer — app com.rerdubai.app

1

From the app (immediate)

Open the Real Estate Referrer app, go to Profile, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account. Confirm when prompted. Your account and your data are deleted straight away and you are signed out. This cannot be undone.

2

By email (if you no longer have the app)

Send us a deletion request from the email address associated with your account. We complete the deletion within 30 days and confirm it by email.

Request deletion by email

The request must come from the address registered on the account, so that we can verify it is really you.

What is deleted

  • • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • • Your profile: name, email address, phone number, postal address
  • • The referrals (leads) you submitted, and their contact details
  • • Your signed Channel Partner Agreement and any uploaded documents
  • • Your notification preferences and push token

What we keep, and for how long

If a commission has already been paid to you, we keep the corresponding accounting records (amount, date, transaction reference) for the period required by UAE law. These records are kept for legal and tax purposes only; they are not used to contact you and are not linked to an active account. Everything else is deleted.

A question before deleting? Write to contact@real-estate-referrer.com. See also our privacy policy.
